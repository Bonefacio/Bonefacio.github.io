Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AnatVet mobile application. AnatVet is a veterinary educational and clinical reference tool. The data handling described below may vary depending on the features you use, your device settings, your consent choices and applicable law.

Publisher and contact

The application publisher is responsible for AnatVet and this Privacy Policy.

AnatVet application publisher
Email: kontakt@anatvet.net

Data stored locally

Depending on how you use the application, AnatVet may store the following information locally on your device:

  • patient details and related clinical entries;
  • notes, reminders and workflow history;
  • saved calculations and dose history;
  • application settings and local preferences.

This user-entered information is stored in the application's local storage on your device and is used to provide the features you request.

Accounts and cloud services

The current version of AnatVet does not require an AnatVet account or login. It does not provide cloud synchronization for patient records, and AnatVet does not operate a server that stores your patient records. If these features are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before they are used.

Advertising

AnatVet may display advertising using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interaction information and diagnostic information. The information processed depends on the advertising configuration, your consent choices, Google settings and applicable legal requirements.

Google acts under its own terms and privacy practices. More information is available in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google AdMob Help Center.

Separation of clinical information and advertising: AnatVet does not intentionally send patient names, patient notes, medical records or other user-entered clinical data to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

Advertising availability may also depend on a user's subscription status. Users with an active Premium entitlement may not be shown advertising.

Advertising consent

Where required by applicable law, the application may request consent for advertising-related data processing before eligible ads are shown. The type of advertising shown may depend on your consent choices, Google's advertising settings and applicable legal requirements, including requirements that apply in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom.

You may also be able to review or change advertising and privacy choices through controls made available in the application, your device, or your Google account.

Subscriptions and purchases

Optional subscriptions may be offered through Google Play. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy practices. AnatVet does not receive your full payment card details. The application may receive purchase status and related transaction identifiers from Google Play to provide or restore Premium access.

Local notifications

AnatVet may use system notification permissions to deliver local reminders created or enabled by you. You can refuse or disable notification permission in your device settings. The application remains usable without notification permission, although reminder alerts may not appear.

PDF export and sharing

You may choose to export information as a PDF file and share it using another application or service. Export and sharing are initiated by you. Once a file is sent outside AnatVet, its subsequent handling is governed by the destination application or service and your choices there.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it using available application functions, clear the application's data through system settings, or uninstall the application. Removing the application may delete locally stored information, subject to your device and backup settings.

Your choices and rights

You can review or delete locally stored information using available application and system functions. You can also manage notification permissions, advertising settings and other device permissions through your device settings. For privacy questions or requests concerning AnatVet, contact kontakt@anatvet.net.

Children's privacy

AnatVet is intended for veterinary education and professional or reference use and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AnatVet's features or data practices change, including changes involving online services, analytics, advertising, payments or cloud synchronization. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.
